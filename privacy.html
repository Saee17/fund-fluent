Privacy

Last updated October 2026.

No account, no personal data

Fund Fluent has no sign-up and does not ask for your name, email or phone number. Your progress (XP, streak, finished lessons, mistakes) is saved only in your own browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data erases it, and you can also erase it from Settings.

Anonymous usage data

If it's switched on, we record anonymous events such as "lesson started", "question answered correctly" or "lesson finished", tagged with the lesson's id. We use PostHog for this. It sets no cookies, records no screens, and we don't send page addresses or anything you type. It's used only to see which lessons are confusing or where people stop. You can switch it off in Settings, and it stays off if your browser sends "Do Not Track".

Reviews

If you rate Fund Fluent, your star rating and optional comment are stored by our host, Netlify. Please don't put personal details in the comment.

Not investment advice

Fund Fluent teaches general concepts about Indian mutual funds, tax and markets. It isn't financial, tax or legal advice. Rules change; check current rules or speak to a registered adviser before acting.
